# Flexible Working Arrangements

This policy governs arrangements under which employees carry out their duties from an alternative work location rather than a designated company office. The company recognises that a distributed workforce can deliver strong results when appropriate conditions are in place. These guidelines ensure that telecommuting arrangements support individual productivity, protect company data, and sustain effective virtual collaboration across teams.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period are eligible to request an off-site schedule. Staff in their initial onboarding phase are required to attend the office in person so that they can build working relationships and familiarise themselves with internal tools and processes before transitioning to any alternative work location arrangement. Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible for telecommuting arrangements without explicit approval from HR.

## Approval and Off-site Scheduling

A request to work from an alternative work location must be submitted in writing to the employee's line manager at least two weeks before the proposed start date. The submission should set out the proposed number of off-site days per week, the intended location, and confirmation that the workspace meets the technical and ergonomic standards in this policy. Line managers are required to respond within five working days. Approved arrangements are recorded in the HR system and are subject to annual review. Arrangements may be adjusted or withdrawn if business needs, team structure, or the employee's circumstances change materially.

## Synchronous Availability

Employees operating on an off-site schedule must be reachable during the core virtual collaboration window of 10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone. This synchronous window enables distributed team members to participate in reviews, planning sessions, and collaborative working without excessive scheduling friction. Outside the core window, employees may structure their working day as they choose, provided that agreed deliverables are completed on time. Employees whose local time zone differs from the team's primary location by more than three hours should agree on an adjusted availability window with their line manager before commencing the off-site arrangement.

## Technology Provision and Allowances

The company provides a laptop and an external monitor to support productive work from an alternative work location. An annual technology and equipment allowance of CHF 300 is available for items such as ergonomic seating, a keyboard, or display accessories. A one-time set-up allowance of CHF 200 is granted when an off-site arrangement is first approved, to cover initial workspace costs such as a mouse, desk lamp, or cable management. All claims require receipts and must be submitted within ninety days of the arrangement being approved. Network connectivity costs are borne by the employee. All company-supplied equipment remains company property and must be returned if the arrangement ends or employment terminates.

## Alternative Workspace Standards

The alternative workspace should provide a stable, dedicated working surface, a chair with appropriate back support, and adequate natural or artificial lighting. Monitor height should be set to eye level to reduce postural strain, and the screen should be positioned to avoid reflective glare. Employees sharing accommodation with others should ensure that confidential screen content is not visible to non-employees during working hours. The company's display screen equipment guidelines, available on the intranet, apply equally to off-site workspaces, and employees are encouraged to use their annual allowance to address any ergonomic shortcomings.

## Information Security

Employees working from an alternative work location must access internal systems exclusively through the company's virtual private network (VPN). Company data must not be downloaded to, or processed on, personal devices or personal cloud storage accounts. Screens must be locked whenever the employee steps away from the workstation. Use of public or shared networks — in cafés, hotels, or co-working spaces — is only permitted when the VPN is active. Any suspected security incident, including loss or theft of a company device, must be reported to IT Security without delay. Failure to follow these requirements may result in the off-site arrangement being suspended pending investigation.
