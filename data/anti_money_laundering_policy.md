# Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Protocol

## 1. Know Your Customer and Identity Verification Procedures
The enterprise implements strict Know Your Customer procedures for all commercial engagements and software acquisitions. Sales operations teams shall utilize the ComplyAdvantage database to verify the identity of corporate clients before signing software licensing agreements or processing financial transfers. Verification mandates identifying the ultimate beneficial owner holding an equity stake of 25% or more in the contracting entity. No client accounts shall activate within the central customer relationship management platform without comprehensive background validation, sanction list checking, and PEP screening. Compliance teams are required to re-verify client documentation on a rolling 12-month schedule to catch corporate restructuring.

## 2. Cash Transaction Thresholds and MROS Reporting Mandates
Transactions involving physical currency are heavily restricted under this anti-money laundering framework to mitigate illicit asset integration. The enterprise enforces a hard ceiling prohibiting physical cash receipts exceeding 15,000 CHF for any single transaction or linked series of payments. Any attempt by external entities to structure payments below this threshold triggers a mandatory compliance review by the legal department. If a transaction exhibits indicators of money laundering or unusual transactional structuring, the Anti-Money Laundering Officer shall file a suspicious activity report with the Money Laundering Reporting Office Switzerland within a strict 24-hour timeframe. The enterprise will cease all interactions with the flagged entity immediately upon filing.

## 3. Politically Exposed Persons and Enhanced Sanctions Screening
Enhanced due diligence applies automatically to all accounts associated with Politically Exposed Persons or organizations located in jurisdictions flagged by the Financial Action Task Force. Screening algorithms execute continuous checks against international sanctions registries every 12 months. When a match occurs, accounts freeze automatically, and funds remain blocked pending FINMA clearance. All verification trails, transactional records, and identity files require secure archival retention for a duration of 7 years following relationship termination. Violations of these AMLA regulations expose individual actors to independent prosecution and institutional fines up to 50,000 CHF, alongside structural license revocation for the enterprise if systematic negligence is identified during audits.

## 4. Internal Audit controls and Transactional Monitoring
To ensure total adherence to statutory regulations, the internal compliance unit performs monthly automated transaction auditing via the Actimize monitor platform. This tool flags erratic wire transfers, anomalous payment volumes, or mismatched banking country codes. Any transaction flag requires manual validation within 5 business days. Personnel who intentionally bypass these controls or engage in tipping-off offences by alerting clients to internal reviews face immediate termination and personal liability under federal statutes. Annual training refreshers are mandatory for all finance, accounting, and sales executives, requiring a minimum passing grade of 90% on the final certification exam.
