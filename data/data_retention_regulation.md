# Data Retention Regulation

This regulation defines how long different categories of data must be kept and when they must be deleted. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who create, process, or store company data. Retention periods are set in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nDSG), and relevant provisions of the Swiss Code of Obligations.

## Employee HR Records

Personnel files — including employment contracts, performance appraisals, salary records, disciplinary documentation, and records of training completion — are retained for ten years from the date of termination of the employment relationship. This period reflects the applicable statute of limitations for employment-related claims under Swiss law. Records relating to workplace accidents or occupational illness are subject to a fifteen-year retention period to satisfy obligations under Swiss accident insurance legislation (UVG).

## Financial Transactions

All records of financial transactions — including invoices, purchase orders, receipts, payment confirmations, and bank statements — are retained for ten years from the close of the relevant financial year. This period is prescribed by Article 958f of the Swiss Code of Obligations and applies equally to paper and electronic records. Payroll records are subject to the same ten-year period. Any financial document subject to an active litigation hold or regulatory inquiry is exempt from standard deletion until the matter is concluded.

## Customer Records

Customer contact details and order history are retained for ten years after the last transaction, as required for tax and accounting purposes. After this period the records are deleted automatically.

## Email Communications

Business email correspondence sent or received via company accounts is retained for three years. Emails that form part of an active legal matter, regulatory inquiry, or formal complaint are placed on a legal hold and must not be deleted until the matter is resolved, regardless of the standard retention period. Employees must not conduct business communications from personal email accounts, as such messages fall outside the company's retention and audit controls.

## Job Applicant Data

Data from unsuccessful job applicants is kept for six months after the hiring decision, in case of disputes. After six months it is permanently erased unless the applicant has given explicit consent to be kept on file for future openings.

## CCTV Footage

Footage from security cameras installed on company premises is retained for thirty days, after which it is automatically overwritten. Where footage has been flagged in connection with a security incident, workplace accident, or disciplinary investigation, it is preserved until the relevant process is concluded. Access to CCTV recordings is restricted to Security, HR, and Legal, and any access must be authorised and logged.

## Audit Logs

System audit logs that record access to personal data are retained for two years and may not be altered. They are used to investigate security incidents. Logs of privileged system access — including administrator actions, changes to access controls, and bulk data exports — are retained for five years in line with information security best practice and regulatory guidance.

## Right to Erasure

Individuals may request deletion of their personal data. Such requests must be handled within thirty days. Data required by law to be retained, such as invoices, is exempt from erasure until its retention period has passed. Where erasure cannot be completed within thirty days due to technical constraints, the individual must be informed of the delay and provided with an estimated completion date.

## Responsibility

Each department head is responsible for ensuring that records within their function are managed in accordance with this regulation. The Data Protection Officer reviews and updates this regulation at least annually, or whenever changes in applicable law or business practice require revision.
