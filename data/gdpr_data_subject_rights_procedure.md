# GDPR Data Subject Rights Processing Procedure

## 1. Data Subject Access Request Intake and Verification
This procedure details how the organization processes requests under Article 15 of the General Data Protection Regulation. Individuals wishing to exercise their right of access request must submit their queries via the dedicated OneTrust privacy portal. The Data Protection Officer executes identity verification using dual-factor authentication protocols within 48 hours of submission. No informational records change hands without validation against government-issued photographic identification to prevent malicious data harvesting, profile spoofing, or unauthorized credential access. This step is mandatory regardless of whether the requestor is a client, former partner, or active platform user.

## 2. Right to Erasure and Portability Processing
When an individual exercises their right to erasure or data portability, specialized data engineering scripts isolate the relevant datasets within the central Snowflake data warehouse and connected production nodes. The right to erasure requires purging target records across all production environments, analytical platforms, and third-party SaaS integrations within a maximum 30-day response deadline. Data portability requests necessitate exporting all individual records into a structured, machine-readable JSON format, encrypted with AES-256 protocols before digital transmission to the verified recipient via a secure download link that expires within 72 hours.

## 3. Exemptions, Extensions, and Supervisory Reporting
For complex cases involving voluminous unstructured data streams or historical backups, the Data Protection Officer is authorized to apply a 2-month extension, notifying the requestor beforehand with an explicit justification. Standard operations dictate a 0 CHF fee for processing standard requests; however, manifestly unfounded or repetitive queries incur a 150 CHF administrative charge to cover infrastructure processing costs. If an individual disputes processing workflows, they retain the right to lodge grievances directly with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner or relevant European supervisory authorities, which could expose the firm to GDPR fines totaling up to 4% of global turnover.

## 4. Data Inventory Mapping and Audit Logs
The Data Protection Officer maintains a comprehensive data inventory map within the Collibra platform to track the lifecycle of incoming informational elements. Every database modification, deletion script execution, or extraction file generation triggers an entry into an unalterable audit log stored on a separate security server. These logs are retained for 5 years to demonstrate regulatory compliance during external privacy assessments. Internal teams are prohibited from modifying these logging systems, and any downtime exceeding 60 minutes must be reported to the technology infrastructure lead immediately.

## 5. Staff Training and Compliance Auditing Cycles
To maintain adherence to changing privacy frameworks, all operations personnel must complete an annual 2-hour data handling module via the internal training platform. This training covers specific scenarios such as recognizing a data subject request hidden within a standard customer support ticket. Compliance audits are executed bi-annually by an external privacy consultancy to verify data mapping accuracy, system deletion performance, and overall processing timelines. Records of these audits are submitted directly to the board of directors to ensure oversight.
