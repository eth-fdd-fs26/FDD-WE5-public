# Remote Work Policy

This policy describes how employees may work from locations other than the company office. It applies to all full-time staff and sets out eligibility criteria, the approval process, equipment entitlements, ergonomic standards, and security obligations.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period of three months are eligible to request remote work. New hires must work from the office during onboarding so they can meet their team and learn internal tools. Remote work is not available to employees on a performance improvement plan unless expressly approved by HR.

## Approval Process

Employees wishing to establish a regular remote working arrangement must submit a written request to their line manager at least two weeks in advance. The request must specify the proposed number of remote days per week, the intended work location, and confirmation that the home environment meets the ergonomic and security requirements set out in this policy. Managers must respond in writing within five working days. Approved arrangements are recorded in the HR system and are reviewed annually. An arrangement may be modified or revoked if performance, security, or operational requirements change.

## Core Collaboration Hours

Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours, from 10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone. Outside of core hours, employees may arrange their schedule freely as long as deliverables are met. Meetings may not be scheduled outside core hours without the explicit agreement of all participants. Employees whose time zone differs from the team's primary location by more than three hours should agree on an adjusted synchronous window with their manager.

## Equipment and Allowances

The company provides a laptop and a monitor for remote work. Employees may claim up to CHF 300 per year for ergonomic equipment such as a chair or desk. A one-time set-up allowance of CHF 200 is available when a remote working arrangement is first established, covering items such as a keyboard, mouse, or desk lighting. Claims must be submitted within ninety days of the arrangement being approved and must be supported by receipts. Internet costs are not reimbursed. All company-provided equipment remains company property and must be returned if the arrangement ends or employment terminates.

## Ergonomic Standards

The home working environment must meet basic ergonomic requirements. Employees should have a stable desk surface, a chair with adequate back support, and sufficient lighting. Monitor height should be adjusted to eye level to minimise neck strain, and the screen should be positioned to avoid glare. Employees are encouraged to take regular short breaks from screen use. The company's display screen equipment guidelines, available on the intranet, apply equally to home working setups, and employees may use the annual ergonomic allowance to address any deficiencies.

## VPN and Security

When working remotely, employees must connect through the company VPN at all times when accessing internal systems, shared drives, or confidential data. The VPN client must be kept up to date; employees should restart their device promptly when IT pushes an update. Confidential documents may not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage accounts. Screens must be locked when left unattended, even briefly. Working from public Wi-Fi networks — such as those in coffee shops or airports — is only permitted with the VPN active. Any suspected security incident, including a lost or stolen device, must be reported to IT Security without delay.
