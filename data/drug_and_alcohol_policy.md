# Substance Misuse and Workplace Sobriety Regulation

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Thresholds and Blood Alcohol Concentration
The organization enforces an absolute zero-tolerance framework regarding substance misuse within all operational zones, laboratories, and client sites. Under standard office operations, the absolute ceiling for permissible blood alcohol concentration is fixed at 0.02%. For individuals occupying safety-critical positions, including server datacenter technicians, hardware testing engineers, and high-voltage electrical experts, the mandatory threshold is strictly 0.00% during operational hours. Consumption of illicit narcotics or unauthorized prescription stimulants is completely prohibited on corporate premises, and violations result in immediate security escort off-site.

## 2. Testing Protocols and Breathalyzer Screening Mechanisms
The People Operations Unit, in conjunction with retained medical officers, retains authorization to conduct random screening based on reasonable suspicion of intoxication or post-incident evaluation. Testing protocols involve non-invasive breathalyzer tests or rapid saliva test kits administered by certified third-party health professionals. If an initial test indicates a breach of limits, a confirmatory laboratory blood analysis executes within a 2-hour window. Refusal to submit to an authorized screening results in immediate 48-hour suspension from all corporate locations and triggers an automatic disciplinary review.

## 3. Rehabilitation Programs and Disciplinary Escalation Pathways
Staff struggling with chronic chemical dependency are eligible to request entry into a corporate-sponsored rehabilitation program. The organization guarantees job security for a 30-day treatment window, provided the individual self-reports before triggering a suspicious incident test or safety violation review. The organization applies a 3-strikes policy for minor behavioral infractions linked to alcohol usage during off-site corporate events. However, showing signs of advanced intoxication while performing safety-critical engineering tasks results in immediate termination of the contractual relationship, with immediate forfeiture of any outstanding performance bonuses up to 15,000 CHF.

## 4. Corporate Event Standards and Vendor Management
During authorized corporate celebrations, internal networking events, or client dinners, alcohol service is heavily regulated. Catering vendors are strictly instructed to limit alcohol service to beer and wine, completely banning high-proof spirits. Non-alcoholic alternatives must constitute at least 50% of the available beverage inventory. Event organizers must arrange complimentary shuttle services or taxi vouchers up to 80 CHF for any participant who requests safe transit. Serving alcohol to minors or interns under the age of 18 is an absolute violation resulting in immediate disciplinary termination for the hosting manager.

## 5. Supervisor Responsibilities and Documentation Rules
Line managers are required to undergo mandatory training every 12 months on identifying early behavioral warning signs of substance misuse, prescription drug abuse, or alcohol impairment in the workplace. If a manager observes suspicious behavior, they must document their specific observations on a standard Incident Evaluation Form within 1 hour before requesting a screening test. Failing to document observations or ignoring visible signs of impairment in safety-critical environments results in immediate administrative demotion for the responsible supervisor.
