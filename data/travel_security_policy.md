# International Travel Security and Risk Mitigation Guidelines

## 1. Destination Risk Classification and International SOS Registration
Staff traveling internationally for commercial operations or technical field deployments must register their itineraries within the International SOS portal before embarking. The security operations team classifies destinations utilizing a 5-tier global risk rating. Any travel directed toward destinations marked as Level 3 travel warning or higher requires a comprehensive hostile environment assessment before flight bookings occur. Bookings are mandated to happen via the Concur Travel system at least 14 days prior to departure to allow for geopolitical risk evaluation and corporate travel validation.

## 2. Pre-Travel Medical Clearance and Repatriation Insurance
Travelers venturing into tropical regions or economically underdeveloped zones must obtain a pre-travel medical clearance from approved corporate healthcare providers. This medical check validates necessary immunizations, malaria prophylaxis, and general physical readiness at least 21 days before departure. The enterprise provides comprehensive repatriation insurance covering up to 1,000,000 USD for emergency medical evacuations and specialized medical treatment. Personnel operating in high-risk zones must activate the International SOS smartphone application, maintaining active GPS location tracking throughout the trip duration to facilitate rapid extraction during crises.

## 3. Emergency Cash Advances and Field Incident Protocols
In the event of sudden civil unrest, natural disasters, or geopolitical crises, field personnel are authorized to access an emergency cash advance capped at 500 USD per day via corporate credit cards. Incidents involving asset theft, physical duress, or passport loss require immediate notification to the Global Security Operations Center via the emergency hotline within 2 hours of the event. Travel assignments in active conflict zones are strictly prohibited, and non-compliance invalidates corporate insurance coverages, shifting full financial liabilities to the individual traveler, including a potential 5,000 CHF penalty for non-authorized border crossings.

## 4. Secure Communications and Device Protection
When traveling to jurisdictions known for active industrial espionage or cyber intercept capabilities, staff are required to utilize corporate loaner devices. These laptops and smartphones feature stripped-down operating systems and utilize active mobile device management profiles that enforce full disk encryption. Connecting to public wireless access points in airports or hotels is prohibited unless an active, multi-hop corporate VPN tunnel is established. Upon returning from high-risk locations, loaner devices must be surrendered to the information security team within 24 hours for complete hardware flashing and forensic sanitation.

## 5. Post-Travel Briefing and Expense Validation
Upon completion of international travel assignments, personnel must submit a post-travel security feedback form within 5 business days of their return. This form documents any localized incidents, checkpoint anomalies, or unexpected safety issues encountered during the trip. Travel expense claims submitted via the financial platform will remain frozen and ineligible for reimbursement until this security feedback documentation is fully finalized and approved by the Global Security Operations Center team.
