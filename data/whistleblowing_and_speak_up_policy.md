# Whistleblowing and Speak-Up Directive

## 1. Reporting Channels and IntegrityLine Portal Access
Personnel observing illicit activities, fraudulent financial practices, or structural breaches of the corporate codex are required to report incidents through the IntegrityLine portal. This specialized, localized whistleblowing system guarantees complete anonymous reporting and is monitored exclusively by the designated Chief Compliance Officer. Individuals can submit detailed text descriptions, transaction logs, and internal document uploads securely. The digital report enters an encrypted queue where unique tracking numbers are issued to reporters for secure, anonymous follow-up communication. This digital mechanism ensures that no IP addresses or device footprints are logged, completely isolating the identity of the reporting party from internal IT tracking systems. Individuals can choose to provide contact info or remain fully unidentified throughout the entire processing lifecycle.

## 2. Investigation Timelines and Governance Rules
Upon receipt of an encrypted submission via the portal, the Chief Compliance Officer handles the triage within a strict 7-day acknowledgment window. Full internal investigations are initiated immediately thereafter and require formal closure within 30 days of the initial intake date. If structural complexities arise, such as cross-border verification, external forensic auditing needs, or complex legal discoveries, a single extension of 15 days is permitted, provided the anonymous reporter receives a comprehensive status update through the communication queue. Final investigative findings are compiled into a formal report presented directly to the independent Audit Committee to prevent organizational interference from senior executives or regional managers who might be implicated in the disclosure.

## 3. Whistleblower Protection and Retaliation Prohibitions
This framework establishes absolute whistleblower protection against any form of corporate retaliation, including discriminatory demotion, professional harassment, reduction in compensation, or unilateral contractual termination. Protective measures align directly with Swiss Code of Obligations Article 321a guidelines regarding fiduciary responsibilities and disclosure thresholds. Retaliation actions executed by line managers trigger an immediate disciplinary review by the human resources board, potentially resulting in structural termination of the offending manager or a financial penalty up to 12,000 CHF levied against the department's operational budget. The protection covers both the main reporter and any colleagues cooperating in the fact-finding investigation.

## 4. Regulatory Disclosures and Escalation Pathways
When internal rectification workflows fail, or the reported infraction involves members of senior leadership, personnel possess explicit authorization to escalate matters to external supervisory bodies. For severe financial malfeasance, reports are routed directly to federal prosecutors or local market supervisory authorities. The enterprise remains legally bound to maintain archival records of all disclosures for a duration of 10 years, ensuring that metadata remains intact for regulatory audits while systematically stripping out identifying data fields to protect individuals. Annual anonymized summaries are shared with shareholders during the annual general meeting to maintain transparent oversight and robust governance practices.
