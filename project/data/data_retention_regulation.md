# Data Retention Regulation

This regulation defines how long different categories of data must be kept and
when they must be deleted. It exists to comply with data protection law.

## Customer Records

Customer contact details and order history are retained for ten years after the
last transaction, as required for tax and accounting purposes. After this period
the records are deleted automatically.

## Job Applicant Data

Data from unsuccessful job applicants is kept for six months after the hiring
decision, in case of disputes. After six months it is permanently erased unless
the applicant has given consent to be kept on file for future openings.

## Right to Erasure

Individuals may request deletion of their personal data. Such requests must be
handled within thirty days. Data required by law to be retained, such as
invoices, is exempt from erasure until its retention period has passed.

## Audit Logs

System audit logs that record access to personal data are retained for two years
and may not be altered. They are used to investigate security incidents.
