# Remote Work Policy

This policy describes how employees may work from locations other than the
company office. It applies to all full-time staff.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period of three months are eligible
to request remote work. New hires must work from the office during onboarding so
they can meet their team and learn internal tools.

## Working Hours

Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours, from 10:00 to
16:00 in their local time zone. Outside of core hours, employees may arrange
their schedule freely as long as deliverables are met.

## Equipment and Expenses

The company provides a laptop and a monitor for remote work. Employees may claim
up to 300 CHF per year for ergonomic equipment such as a chair or desk. Internet
costs are not reimbursed.

## Data Security

When working remotely, employees must use the company VPN to access internal
systems. Confidential documents may not be stored on personal devices, and
screens must be locked when left unattended.
